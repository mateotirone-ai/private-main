# Design patterns (Economy World adapted)

Source ideas from wzhaoMS building patterns, retargeted from modern city
megastructures to soft-stylized trade/civic buildings.

## Massing families

Pick one primary family per building; do not mash all of them:

- **Hall** — wide front, shallow depth, strong gable or hip to the street
  (bakery, general store, town office).
- **Workshop** — longer under a ridge, side yard or rear lean-to
  (sawmill, smeltery, forge).
- **Yard works** — compact office/storefront + open work volume
  (quarry gatehouse, lumber camp, fishery dock shed).
- **Stacked civic** — two clear floors, balcony or colonnade optional
  (bank, real estate, town hall wings).

Keep the street face calm; put mess/work volume to the side or rear so the
town reads intentional.

## Depth and silhouette

1. **Foundation band** — 1-block skirt or stone plinth different from walls.
2. **Wall field** — primary material; break every bay.
3. **Frame** — logs, stripped logs, or stone pillars at corners and door.
4. **Openings** — inset 1 block when pad allows; trapdoors/shutters as eyelids.
5. **Eaves** — slabs/stairs overhang; never a flat top on a "roofed" building.
6. **Crown** — chimney, hoist, roof lantern, weather vane — one skyline tick.

## Facade rhythm

- Choose a bay width (usually 3 or 4 blocks) and stick to it.
- Door bay is wider or framed harder than window bays.
- Mirror left/right only when the program is symmetrical; workshops often
  should be asymmetric (door + loading).
- Awnings (stairs/slabs + fence posts) help storefronts read as shops.

## Trade landmarks (one hero per building)

| Trade vibe | Landmark ideas |
|---|---|
| Bakery | Chimney + warm interior glow, display counter to street |
| Smith / smeltery | Chimney stack, dark palette, tool racks, ember lighting |
| Sawmill / lumber | Log stacks, saw frame, open lean-to |
| Fishery | Dock planks, hanging nets (cobweb carefully), barrels |
| Quarry / mine | Crane/hoist, cut stone piles, rugged gate |
| Store | Awning, crate stack, wide window, sign board |

If you cannot name the hero landmark, the build will look generic.

## Interiors

- Counter or workbench visible from entrance when the building sells/services.
- Office anchor on a clear floor tile, not inside furniture.
- Storage as secondary room or loft — do not clog the door path.
- Ceiling height: 3–4 clear blocks for cozy; raise for forges/halls.

## Lighting

- Warm interior: lanterns, frogs lights sparingly, hidden glowstone under
  carpets/trapdoors only when needed.
- Exterior: one lantern at the door, optional path lights — avoid airport
  runways of torches.
- Night silhouette should still show the roof and landmark.

## Town / street realism (scaled down)

From city-realism research, keep only what fits a cozy town:

- Varied neighbor heights when placing multiple buildings.
- Alleys as rare service gaps, not every lot.
- Street furniture light touch: barrel, bench, crate — not cars/subways.
- Gaps between pads matter; do not weld buildings into one mega-block.

## Tier upgrades without pad growth

- **L1** — honest shell, clear function, minimal props.
- **L2** — better materials, deeper facade, fuller interior, stronger landmark.
- **L3** — ornament + capacity cues (extra chimney, loft, covered yard), same
  gate and front.

## Anti-patterns

- Wool rainbow walls
- Flat roof on a medieval/timber fantasy building unless intentional modern era
- Door with no path to the street
- Windows painted on with buttons only (use actual openings or deep frames)
- Copy-pasted interior of chests with no circulation
- Skyscraper curtain walls for Settlement/Village era trades
