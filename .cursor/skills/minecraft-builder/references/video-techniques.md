## Grian — How NOT to Build (`HUzggMjVze8`)

Cardinal sins:

1. **One-block material** — eyes have nothing to break on.
2. **Flat walls** — push wall back, frame outside, prefer panes; add roof overhang.
3. **Bad palette** — clashing precious/random blocks.
4. **Rectangle-only shape** — varied footprint beats identical detailing on a box.
   Experiment: same palette + detail on rectangle vs irregular plan → irregular wins;
   jutting volumes funnel eye to the main mass.
5. **Unstable decorative water** — ponds must be still/finished.
6. **Only vanilla trees** — handmade trees with messy canopies elevate houses.
7. **2-high huts** — raise clearance so interiors are usable.
8. **Over-detailing** — slab/stair spam that reads as noise; simpler structured
   walls with clear windows/outlines often look more skilled.
9. **Unsupported right-angle overhangs** — add posts/frames; dress with stairs/slabs.
10. **Flat-topped tall boxes** — add any competent roof (slab peak, stair gable,
    mixed stair/full patterns).

Takeaway: avoid sins and shape/depth/palette improve even without a block-by-block
tutorial.

## Grian — 5 Easy Steps to Improve Your House (`TRVIFdGT1eY`)

1. **Outer frame** — posts diagonal outside corners; horizontal beams; multi-story
   mid beams. Frame is outside the wall plane.
2. **Change shape** with the frame — knock corners for balconies, extend reception
   volumes; fill walls inside the frame.
3. **Roof** — overhang first all edges; fill; match roof to the house (don’t
   copy one recipe blindly).
4. **Light detailing** — windows per wall segment; sparse fence/stair depth.
5. **Transition to ground** — garden/farm/path so the house spreads into terrain
   instead of sticking out of a flat plane.

## elodie — Why Hermitcraft builds look good (`SsXt23bd8ik`)

Composition for builds (not only screenshots):

- **Primary viewing angle** — design the street approach on purpose.
- **Rule of thirds / weight** — balance mass; off-center organic forms.
- **Leading lines** — corners and edges arrow the eye into the entry.
- **Symmetry** for monumental fronts; organic asymmetry for others.
- **Detail gradient with height** — denser detail near eye level; simplify upward
  so tall builds feel taller.
- **Narrative wear** — intentional inconsistencies, vines, burnt gradients as story.
- Camera aids (FOV/zoom/fog) are presentation; still design for the walk-up angle.

## soujju — 25 tips (`1p_DylEdXEE`)

High-signal practical list:

- No dirt under houses; extend foundation / cover.
- Replace blocks beside paths/farmland for cleaner ground joints.
- Unexpected blocks as texture (hay thatch, composters as paneling).
- Fence/wall props under overhangs; fence-gate small windows.
- **Odd-number plans** for roof centers.
- **Trapdoors everywhere** — floors, planters, pillar caps, shutters, shelves,
  ceilings, arches, hidden lights.
- Terrain-led siting; not superflat plop.
- Plan as big box + smaller additive boxes; main roof on biggest box first.
- Texture with 2–3 close-shade blocks, not rainbow noise.
- Recess doors; upside-down stair headers.
- If wall is flat, add frames/planters/leaves — anything with depth.
- Landscape until finished; leaf litter, moss patches, replant trees.
- ~5 block wall height sweet spot for small houses (door + centered window).
- Roof join method: finish main, then secondary frames into it.
- Even-width emergency: sideways slab half-triangle ridge.
- Dormers shorter than main roof.
- String to freeze plant growth / reduce spawns; shears to lock glowberries.
- Sapling-assisted custom trees + stair/trapdoor trunks.
- Scale props to building size.
- Hide lights behind trapdoors; stair “wear” and waterlogged cracks for age.
